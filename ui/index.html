Let a little world happen.

Doodles create useful houses, farms, tools and weapons. Each job needs learned skills and real materials. They experiment or learn from skilled neighbours before advanced work unlocks. Conversations create trust; conflict creates anger and grudges. Doodles choose whether to found, join, refuse or leave circles. Membership is reconsidered from trust, values, loyalty, independence and danger. Food, water, ink, weather and the day/night cycle affect their choices.

Doodles choose what to do from their personality, needs and previous outcomes. They begin as novices. Households need beds and supplies. Food shortages motivate farms; fields need water and time before harvesting. Learned skills, crafting and teaching create roles. Leaders need support; campaigns need trained, equipped and provisioned followers. Territory can change after sustained occupation. Rallies can be refused. Children inherit genes and visual traits; skills begin at zero. Deaths are permanent within the saved world, and family stories and drawings remain. Learned construction, farming, crafting, foraging and combat improve their effectiveness.

This is a local simulation with procedural creativity, rule-based emotions, experience-based action values and skills. It does not train a neural network. Groq is an optional high-level planner.
