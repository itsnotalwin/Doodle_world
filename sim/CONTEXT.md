# Simulation
`engine.js` owns fixed-step activity, experience learning, construction, conflict and version 3 saves. `world-systems.js` owns biomes, resources, social interactions, art composition and globe math. `drawing-library.js` provides 83 new original vector motifs in addition to the original 12. `life-systems.js` owns 24 traits, paired genes/phenotype, ancestry, birth/death, individual membership evaluation and voluntary rally support. No DOM/network. Build embeds modules in dependency order. Keep state finite, bounded and serializable; deceased records preserve ownership and family links. v1/v2 migration preserves existing sketches/skills. Tests cover migration, affiliation/refusal/exit, offspring, killing/destruction/upgrades, long runs and globe projection. Creativity and learning are procedural, not neural training.
